Auto-detects variants, marks Bountiful delves, and click to open delve on the map.

Difficulty research (Midnight delves)

Ratings (S = Turbo/fastest through D = Very slow) come from a community tier list, variant-specific callouts, beta and forum feedback, and a check of blue posts and hotfixes.

S-tier: Atal'Aman (eagle route skip, arguably fastest run in the expansion) and Sunkiller Sanctum (portal traversal, manageable pulls). A-tier: The Darkway (Arcway-style, simple mechanics), Shadowguard Point (outdoor, mount, straightforward boss), The Grudge Pit (one of the easier Midnight delves). B-tier: Parhelion Plaza (middle-of-the-pack), The Gulf of Memory (vines/spores puzzle slows clear), The Shadow Enclave (overall B; Mirror Shine is the slow variant). C-tier: Collegiate Calamity (dense, Lightbloom DoT, Hydrangea roots) and Twilight Crypts (survival, invuln roamers, levers, lethal boss frontal).

Variant callouts: Bombing Run (Parhelion) was cited as slowest of the three, so D. Mirror Shine (Shadow Enclave) had beta feedback “over an hour” and “actively avoid when mirror mechanic,” so D. Leyline Technician (The Darkway) is slower due to crystal/backtrack, B; the other two A. Faculty of Fear (Collegiate) and Loosed Loa (Twilight) are the slowest variant in their delves, D. Where we had no variant-specific info, we used the general delve tier for that story.

Beta and forum: Feedback: Delves (Midnight Beta) mentioned Twilight Crypts gates/objectives and Valeera positioning, Shadow Enclave exit to Hillsbrad, Parhelion ~10 min at T11, and The Darkway 20+ min with the crystal objective. Delves in Hindsight (General Discussion) reported T11 ~15–20 min solo depending on gear/companion, with Brann/Valeera level and gear making a big difference.

Blue posts and hotfixes: We didn’t find any Midnight-specific delve difficulty hotfixes (Dec 2025–Feb 2026; only a Bountiful loot fix). TWW had Sep 2024 scaling and “Lives Remaining” tuning; that doesn’t apply to Midnight’s delve list.

Ratings live in Data.lua and can be tuned from experience.
